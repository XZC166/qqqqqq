# Early-period coverage audit

This audit reports the reviewed private collection as of 2026-11-10. Counts refer to formal unique fulltexts, not the acquisition working pool.

| Publication period | Formal records | American Presidency Project | Share | Media | Years with no formal record |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1949-1972 | 797 | 739 | 92.72% | 48 | None |
| 1973-1991 | 761 | 746 | 98.03% | 13 | None |

Annual record presence does not establish annual source diversity. In 1949-1972, ten years have no formal media records. In 1973-1991, media records occur only in 1979 and 1989. No contemporaneous independent polling organization is represented in the formal early-period set; CBS transcript records do not substitute for VOA/USIA institutional material.

The period totals are heavily concentrated in presidential documents hosted by the American Presidency Project. Multiple copies or archival manifestations of government statements are not independent corroboration. These counts therefore support analysis of U.S. executive discourse more strongly than cross-media reception, public opinion, or broadcast-policy claims.

Coverage review indexed ten event windows: the founding of the PRC, Korean War, both Taiwan Strait crises, China's first nuclear test, the opening and Nixon visit, normalization and the Taiwan Relations Act, the 1982 communique, 1989 protests/sanctions, and the Cold War's end. The event index uses publication dates and literal body clues to locate material. It does not establish causality or prove that a thesis claim has independent corroboration. Researchers should compare government, press, broadcast and polling records for each claim and preserve contrary evidence.

## Collection priorities

- Locate original contemporaneous poll releases, questionnaires and publication dates around normalization and 1989. Later Gallup/Pew trend pages are discovery aids, not contemporaneous publications, and one survey cited by multiple later summaries remains one survey.
- Locate complete, dated VOA/USIA policy reports, audience research, broadcast scripts and translations. NARA Record Group 306 finding aids establish that relevant series exist, but an archival description is not a source text.
- Add contemporaneous independent reporting around the 1954-1955 and 1958 Taiwan Strait crises, 1971-1972 opening, 1978-1979 normalization and 1982 communique, and 1989 protests and sanctions.

All full texts and evidence remain private research copies. Existing pending, restricted and retraction disclosures remain in force. This audit does not claim exhaustive coverage or sufficient cross-source corroboration.
