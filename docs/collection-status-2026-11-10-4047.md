The current regression suite has 626 passing tests, including date conflicts, missing source body blocks, truncated documents, missing declared pages and source evidence changes.

The active source queues include presidential compilations, Congressional Record granules, public government briefing/speech archives, think tanks and additional early presidential materials. Daily Compilation dates explicitly labelled Event Date remain pending without independent publication-date evidence. FRUS volume XML remains separate where exact publication days are unestablished; its historical datelines are not publication evidence.

Early media and VOA archives, restricted major media, official X/YouTube text, Chinese comparative sources and authorized literature databases remain coverage gaps, not excluded sources. A social post is its own textual publication, not the full linked article. Video summaries do not qualify as complete transcripts. There is no claim of exhaustion or a collection plateau.

Workbooks, full texts, raw captures, databases and review manifests remain private research artifacts. This Git checkpoint contains code, synthetic regression tests and aggregate reporting only; it grants no permission to publicly redistribute source texts.
